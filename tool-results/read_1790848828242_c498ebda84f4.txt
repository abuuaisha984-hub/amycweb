It was established in **1980** [VERIFIED from the "Since 1980" achievement stat] with the mission of reviving the authentic teachings of Islam and fostering the holistic development of youth within the faith. Over more than four decades of service, AMYC has grown into a multi-programme institution operating a nationwide network of schools, regional branches (*Majimbo*), community welfare initiatives, healthcare contributions, orphan care programmes, and an in-house media and communications arm — including its own radio station, Radio Ihsaan FM.

    72→    72→## 2. Programmes & Services
    73→    73→
    74→    74→The live site publishes **six core service areas**. The AMYC brief lists eight activity areas; the additional two (Social Welfare, Youth Development, Development Projects) are reconstructed here as natural groupings of the verified six. Each description below blends the verified homepage/service-page text with professional elaboration.
    75→    75→
    76→    76→### 2.1 Da'wah Efforts  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site]
    77→    77→AMYC warmly invites people to learn about Islam through classes, lectures, and community events that share the Islamic message in a clear and welcoming way, based on the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah. The Da'wah programme is the spiritual backbone of the Centre — covering mosque-based teaching, public lectures, study circles (*halaqah*), printed and digital outreach, and the training of du'aat and imaams. *Swahili: Mihadhara, makongamano, na mafunzo ya kuiweka wazi dalili ya Uislamu kwa jamii.*
    78→    78→
    79→    79→### 2.2 Education (Educational Initiatives)  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site]
    80→    80→AMYC puts a major focus on learning. The Centre runs more than 25 schools across Tanzania — both Islamic studies (Maahad) and general/environmental schools — teaching Islamic studies alongside environmental awareness, from kindergarten all the way to university level. AMYC's educational institutions aim to graduate youth who are simultaneously grounded in authentic Islamic knowledge and competent in modern academic disciplines. *Swahili: Elimu ya Kiislamu na ya kisasa kuanzia chekechea hadi chuo kikuu.*
    81→    81→
    82→    82→### 2.3 Social Welfare  [RECONSTRUCTED — the live site groups this under "Community Services" and "Orphan Welfare"]
    83→    83→AMYC extends practical social support to individuals and families, including matrimonial services, funeral arrangements, support for the poor and needy, zakat and sadaqah distribution, and emergency relief. The welfare arm works hand-in-hand with AMYC's Majimbo (regional branches) to identify and respond to local needs in a dignified, culturally appropriate manner. *Swahili: Huduma za kijamii kwa wajane, mayatima, na familia dhaifu.*
    84→    84→
    85→    85→### 2.4 Community Services  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site, plus homepage list]
    86→    86→AMYC goes beyond religious teaching, offering practical community support that includes matrimonial services, funeral arrangements, well drilling (clean water), mosque construction, and related community infrastructure. By directly addressing day-to-day community needs, AMYC builds trust and lasting social impact in the regions it serves. *Swahili: Ujenzi wa misikiti, visima, na huduma za haraka kwa jamii.*
    87→    87→
    88→    88→### 2.5 Healthcare Contributions  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site]
    89→    89→AMYC cares for community health by running clinics that operate in line with Islamic principles, offering a unique approach to well-being that integrates physical health and Islamic values. The programme includes preventive health education, basic curative services, maternal and child health outreach, and health camps organized through the Majimbo. *Swahili: Zahanati na huduma za afya zinazolingana na mafunzo ya Kiislamu.*
    90→    90→
    91→    91→### 2.6 Youth Development  [RECONSTRUCTED — central to AMYC's stated mission and "Youth Empowerment" reason-to-choose]
    92→    92→AMYC focuses on empowering youth, providing them with opportunities for personal growth, leadership development, and skill-building. Programmes include leadership training, mentorship, vocational skills, sports and recreation, and Islamic character-building retreats — preparing young Muslims to be confident, ethical, and contributing members of society. *Swahili: Uwezeshaji vijana kupitia uongozi, stadi za kazi, na malezi ya Kiislamu.*
    93→    93→
    94→    94→### 2.7 Development Projects  [RECONSTRUCTED — extension of verified Community Services (well drilling, mosque construction) into broader community development]
    95→    95→AMYC implements community development projects that improve living standards in underserved areas, including water and sanitation infrastructure (boreholes and wells), educational facilities, mosque construction and rehabilitation, and environmental conservation initiatives linked to its "Environmental Schools" network. Projects are delivered in partnership with local communities, donors, and government authorities. *Swahili: Miradi ya maendeleo ya jamii: maji, misikiti, shule, na mazingira.*
    96→    96→
    97→    97→### 2.8 Media & Communication  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site]
    98→    98→AMYC uses the power of radio and social media to share its message. The Centre operates its own radio station, **Radio Ihsaan FM**, and is active across online platforms, producing lectures, news, educational content, and announcements that extend AMYC's reach beyond its physical locations. *Swahili: Redio Ihsaan FM na majukwaa ya mtandaoni kwa uenezi wa risala.*
    99→    99→
   100→   100→### 2.9 Orphan Welfare  [VERIFIED — verbatim description on live site]
   101→   101→AMYC opens its heart and home to orphans, caring for **over 600 children** [VERIFIED stat] by providing a safe and loving environment to grow, along with strong education. The orphan-care programme covers shelter, food, schooling, healthcare, and Islamic upbringing, with the goal of producing self-reliant, righteous young adults. *Swahili: Malezi na elimu kwa mayatima zaidi ya 600.*
